ABOUT THIS SANCTUARY

Poetry By Poets is a bilingual literary sanctuary dedicated to preserving and celebrating timeless Urdu and English poetry-spanning sacred praise of the Creator, devotion to the Final Messenger ﷺ, the unconditional mercy of mothers, and the quiet depths of self-reflection.
